# Expanded free dofollow directory catalog

Research baseline: **2026-10-30**. This catalog records **30 additional distinct domains: three without a reciprocal badge and 27 with a badge or approved backlink requirement**. It supplements the original seeds and general software routes in [the directory playbook](directories.md).

These are dated discovery leads with a published $0 route and operator-policy or sample-link evidence. They are not preapproved destinations, guaranteed current offers, accepted listings or earned backlinks. Recheck the live policy and form before each campaign. The user may request discovery only; this catalog does not authorize publication, accounts, votes, comments or badge deployment.

Selected routes were rechecked during execution on **2026-11-02**. The rows below include newly observed eligibility and participation gates; other baseline observations remain dated October 30. Check domain-rating thresholds before preparing a long application. A directory's own checker can provide an eligibility result; record its reported metric and source without treating a saved draft as a submitted listing.

## Evidence method

- Use aggregators to find leads, then verify cost, eligibility and return-link conditions on the operator's own pricing, submission, FAQ or badge page. Record the URL and check date. A page requesting a dofollow link *to the directory* does not prove that the directory links back as dofollow.
- Separate $0 with no reciprocal obligation, $0 with an ongoing badge/backlink, engagement or winner gates, expiring listings and paid-only routes. Free submission, free product usage and a free trial are different claims.
- Record evidence as **operator policy**, **rendered sample link**, or **the startup's actual published link**. Do not infer a sample's free entitlement when its purchase tier is unknown. Count a newly earned backlink only after checking the actual startup page and target.
- Inspect the actual outbound anchor and its destination. An ordinary link need not contain `rel="dofollow"`; `noopener` and `noreferrer` alone do not make it nofollow. Record `nofollow`, `ugc` and `sponsored` separately. Link attributes do not establish indexing, traffic or ranking credit; keep robots/indexing checks separate.
- Read the active plan, not just flattened text from a pricing grid: crossed-out paid features can appear as ordinary text. Resolve conflicting FAQs, confirm the $0 option, and keep paid fallbacks outside a free campaign.
- Record required badge location, server-rendering/detectability, recheck cadence, removal consequences, listing expiry and renewal cost. Do not assume a separate badge page satisfies a homepage checker.
- Deduplicate by canonical domain and existing product record. Note cross-listing networks and shared templates; distinct domains do not establish independent audiences or common ownership.

## Shared relationships and execution order

IndieLineup, SaaSLineup, TheDevToolsList, TheDevToolsIndex, TheMicroSaaSDir and TheAIToolsDir share a closely similar pricing/submission template and cross-listing pattern with TheSaaSDir and TheDevToolsDir. TheAIToolsDir explicitly identifies TheSaaSDir as a sister directory. Common ownership across all domains was not independently established. First Look and Dofollow.Tools cross-promote directories; paid cross-directory packages are separate from their individual $0 routes.

Start with relevant no-badge routes, then product-category matches whose badge obligations the owner accepts. Account for long queues: SaaSFame and ToolFame advertised up to 180 days. Confirm SaaSaur's inconsistent policy and PublishYourSaaS's selected $0 plan before recording follow success. Use current codehype.ai policy rather than legacy-domain prices.

Keep research, submission receipt, editorial approval, public availability, deployed badge, actual return-link attributes and indexing as independent ledger facts. A list of 30 researched domains must never become a report of 30 new backlinks without those checks.
